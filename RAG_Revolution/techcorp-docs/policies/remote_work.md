# Remote Work Policy

TechCorp allows employees to work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Employees must obtain approval from their manager before working remotely. Remote work arrangements should be documented with HR.

Employees must be available during core working hours, from 10 AM to 3 PM in their local timezone.

A reliable internet connection and a suitable home office setup are required. Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal company systems from home.

Remote work does not change employee performance expectations or responsibilities.
